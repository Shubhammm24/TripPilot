# Packing Guides

## Universal Packing Checklist

### Documents & Money
- Passport / ID (with 6+ months validity)
- Travel insurance documents
- Flight/hotel confirmation printouts
- Credit/debit cards (notify bank of travel dates)
- Small amount of local currency for arrival
- Photocopies of all important documents

### Tech & Gadgets
- Phone + charger + portable battery pack
- Universal power adapter
- Headphones / earbuds
- Camera (if separate from phone)
- E-reader or tablet for long flights

### Health & Toiletries
- Prescription medications (in original packaging)
- Sunscreen SPF 30+
- Insect repellent (for tropical destinations)
- Hand sanitizer
- Basic first-aid items
- Reusable water bottle

## Packing by Climate

### Tropical / Beach (e.g., Bali, Cancun, Goa)
- Lightweight, breathable clothing
- Swimsuits (2-3)
- Reef-safe sunscreen
- Water shoes / sandals
- Light rain jacket (tropical showers)
- Mosquito repellent with DEET
- Quick-dry towel

### Cold Weather (e.g., Iceland, Switzerland, Hokkaido)
- Thermal base layers
- Insulated waterproof jacket
- Warm hat, gloves, scarf
- Waterproof boots with good grip
- Hand warmers
- Lip balm and moisturizer (dry air)

### Urban / City Break (e.g., Paris, Tokyo, NYC)
- Comfortable walking shoes (broken in!)
- Smart casual outfits for restaurants
- Day backpack / crossbody bag
- Umbrella (compact)
- Layers for changing temperatures

### Adventure / Hiking (e.g., Patagonia, Nepal, New Zealand)
- Hiking boots (broken in well before trip)
- Moisture-wicking base layers
- Rain shell jacket + pants
- Trekking poles (if serious hikes)
- Headlamp + extra batteries
- Dry bags for electronics

## Packing Tips

- **Roll, don't fold** — saves space and reduces wrinkles
- **Packing cubes** — game-changer for organization
- **Wear bulkiest items** on the plane (boots, jacket)
- **3-1-1 rule** for carry-on liquids: 3.4oz containers, 1 quart bag, 1 per person
- **Leave 20% space** in your suitcase for souvenirs
- **Pack a change of clothes** in carry-on in case luggage is delayed
