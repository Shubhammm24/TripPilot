# Travel Safety Tips

## General Safety

- Always keep digital and physical copies of your passport, visa, and travel insurance
- Share your itinerary with a trusted person back home
- Register with your country's embassy when traveling to high-risk areas
- Keep emergency numbers saved: local police, ambulance, embassy
- Use a money belt or hidden pouch for valuables in crowded areas

## Scam Awareness

- **Taxi scams**: Always use metered taxis or ride-sharing apps. Agree on fare before getting in
- **Fake tickets**: Only buy attraction tickets from official websites or ticket offices
- **Friendship bracelet scam**: In tourist areas, decline "free" bracelets/gifts — payment will be demanded
- **Distraction theft**: Be wary of people creating distractions (spilling something, asking to sign petitions)
- **Currency exchange scams**: Use ATMs from reputable banks, avoid street exchangers

## Health & Medical

- Check CDC travel health notices before departure
- Pack a basic first-aid kit: bandages, antiseptic, pain relievers, anti-diarrheal
- Stay hydrated — especially in tropical climates
- Know if tap water is safe at your destination; when in doubt, drink bottled
- Carry prescription medications in original packaging with doctor's note

## Digital Safety

- Use a VPN on public Wi-Fi networks
- Enable two-factor authentication on all accounts before traveling
- Back up your phone data to cloud before departure
- Keep offline copies of maps and important documents
- Disable auto-connect to Wi-Fi networks

## Region-Specific Tips

### Southeast Asia
- Be cautious of motorbike bag-snatching in cities like Ho Chi Minh and Bangkok
- Negotiate tuk-tuk prices before boarding
- Beware of gem/suit shop scams ("special sale today only")

### Europe
- Watch for pickpockets at major tourist sites and on public transit
- Some cities have tourist tax — budget €1-5 per night extra
- Avoid unofficial "guides" at major monuments

### South America
- Use radio taxis or apps rather than hailing on the street
- Don't wear flashy jewelry or display expensive electronics
- Learn basic Spanish/Portuguese phrases for emergencies

### Middle East
- Respect local dress codes — cover shoulders and knees in religious sites
- Haggling is expected in souks/markets — start at 50% of asking price
- Carry cash for small purchases; not all vendors accept cards
