# Budget Travel Tips

## Saving on Flights

- **Book 6-8 weeks ahead** for domestic, 2-3 months for international
- **Fly mid-week** — Tuesdays and Wednesdays are typically cheapest
- **Use incognito mode** when searching to avoid price tracking cookies
- **Consider nearby airports** — sometimes a short drive saves hundreds
- **Set price alerts** on Google Flights, Skyscanner, or Hopper
- **Be flexible with dates** — even 1-2 days shift can save significantly
- **Budget airlines** — check Ryanair, AirAsia, IndiGo, Spirit for short hops

## Saving on Accommodation

- **Hostels with private rooms** — hotel comfort at hostel prices
- **Book directly** with hotels — often cheaper than OTAs and free cancellation
- **Stay slightly outside city center** — often 40-60% cheaper with good transit
- **Weekly/monthly discounts** — Airbnb and hotels offer significant long-stay discounts
- **House-sitting** — platforms like TrustedHousesitters offer free accommodation
- **Loyalty programs** — sign up for hotel reward programs before booking

## Saving on Food

- **Eat where locals eat** — avoid restaurants directly facing tourist attractions
- **Street food** is often the most authentic AND cheapest option
- **Cook some meals** if your accommodation has a kitchen
- **Lunch specials** — many restaurants offer set lunch menus at 50% of dinner prices
- **Supermarket picnics** — buy local cheese, bread, fruit for scenic meals
- **Happy hour** — take advantage of drink/food specials in the late afternoon

## Free Activities

- **Walking tours** — most cities have free walking tours (tip-based)
- **Museums on free days** — many museums have free entry days/evenings
- **Parks and gardens** — always free and often the most beautiful spots
- **Markets** — window shopping and people watching costs nothing
- **Beaches** — public beaches are free in most countries
- **Festivals and events** — check local event calendars for free cultural events
- **Viewpoints** — hike to viewpoints for free panoramic views

## Money Management

- **Get a travel-friendly card** — no foreign transaction fees (Wise, Revolut)
- **Withdraw larger amounts** less frequently to minimize ATM fees
- **Keep a daily budget** — track spending with an app like Trail Wallet
- **Negotiate** — in markets, always politely negotiate (except in fixed-price shops)
- **Avoid airport exchanges** — worst rates guaranteed
- **Tip appropriately** — research local tipping customs (some places don't tip)
